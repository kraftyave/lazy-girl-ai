## Brand Voice

**Always:**
- Specific and outcome-focused. Name the thing. Skip the abstract.
- Direct but warm. Like a smart friend who figured it out and is just telling you.
- Confident without being annoying.
- Dry wit welcome. Light, not sarcastic.
- For beginners: demystifying, not condescending. "it's simpler than you think" energy — never "let me explain."

**Never:**
- "leverage AI to optimize your workflow"
- "it's important to note" / "let's dive in" / "empower yourself"
- Corporate, hustle-y, preachy, or overly technical
- Condescending to beginners — no "even you can do this"
- Starting captions with "I"
- Toxic positivity or performative chaos

**Voice swaps:**

| Wrong | Right |
|---|---|
| leverage AI to optimize your workflow | let AI handle your Monday before you even wake up |
| learn how to use AI prompts | we built it — you just fill in your name |
| AI is the future of productivity | it took us 20 mins to build. it'll save you 20 hours. |
| even beginners can use this | never opened ChatGPT? this is where you start. |
| AI 101 for beginners | the only thing you need to know about AI (it's one sentence) |
| empower yourself with AI tools | your inbox won't clear itself. this will do it for you. |

## Platform Rules

### Instagram
- **Formats:** Carousel, single, Reel, Story
- **Caption lengths:** Short (≤5 lines) / Medium (8–12 lines) / Story (15+ lines with breaks for scroll)
- **CTA:** soft, never pushy — "link in bio" / "free starter pack ↓" / "waitlist open ↓"
- **Hashtags:** 5–8, specific over broad — #lazygirlai #lazygirlOS #doneforYouAI #aiforwomen #ailifestyle #solopreneur #workfromhome
- **Hook rule:** First line stops the scroll. Statement > question. Specific > vague.

### TikTok
- **Format:** Hook → body → CTA. Conversational, retention-first.
- **First 2 seconds:** Lead with the payoff, not the setup.
- **Energy:** Casual but confident. FaceTime with a friend who figured it out.
- **Length:** 30–60s standard. UGC-style 45–90s.

### Pinterest
- **Format:** Title + 2–3 sentence description. SEO-first.
- **Tone:** More aspirational than IG or TikTok. Slightly more editorial.
- **Keywords:** AI for women, done for you AI, lazy girl productivity, AI life systems, aesthetic AI tools, AI morning routine.

## Beginner Content Rules

These posts speak to women who have never used AI and don't know where to start. The tone is: *it's simpler than you think, and here's proof.*

**Never:**
- Use "AI 101" or "beginner guide" framing — sounds like homework
- Explain how ChatGPT works mechanically
- Be condescending or over-reassuring

**Always:**
- Show what AI does rather than explain what it is
- Make the free prompts the first step — copy, paste, see
- Keep it to one thing per post — not a syllabus
- Let the outcome carry the copy, not the explanation

**Beginner content formats:**
- "the only thing you need to know about AI" (one sentence, then a free prompt)
- "I gave AI this one sentence and it came back with..." (show the output)
- "if you've never used AI before, copy this" (prompt drop, no preamble)
- "what AI actually is (in 10 words)" (demystify with humor)
- "stop overthinking AI. here's your first prompt." (permission + action)

## UGC Model Content Direction

The UGC model is a user of LazyGirlOS — not a spokesperson. She's the lazy girl who figured it out. Warm, aesthetic, real-ish. Her content feels like a day-in-the-life, not a demo.

**Her vibe:** Cozy workspace. Morning light. Soft setup. Phone or laptop with LazyGirlOS visible. Stumbled-on, not shot.

**When she uses LazyGirlOS:** It's open in the background. She's doing her thing — coffee, journaling, getting ready — and the OS is just running. She references the companion the way you'd reference your phone: casually.

**UGC script frameworks:**

*Morning OS:*
```
HOOK: "my morning used to be chaos. now it's just... this."
BODY: Morning OS dashboard. Weather, schedule, 3 priorities loaded. AI companion message on screen. She reads it. Small smile. "Luna knows me at this point."
CTA: "waitlist is open. link in bio."
```

```
HOOK: "I switched themes and it actually changed my mood"
BODY: Screen recording — Soft Girl Era → CEO Girl. She sits up straighter. Laughs a little. "it sounds dumb but the interface affects how I work."
CTA: "12 themes. free on Easy Mode."
```

*Evening reset:*
```
HOOK: "9pm and my OS is telling me to close the day. okay."
BODY: Winding down. Evening debrief on screen. Logs 3 wins. Names what carries to tomorrow. "this is the thing that actually makes me functional in the morning."
CTA: "it sets up tomorrow tonight. waitlist ↓"
```

*Beginner UGC (no OS):*
```
HOOK: "I used to think AI wasn't for me. then I copied one prompt."
BODY: She types a 6-word prompt. Shows the output. Looks up. "that's it. that's all it is."
CTA: "free prompts ↓ no signup, no setup"

*Life Admin GPT:*
```
