## Hero

### `hero-reel.mp4`
Looping background video for the hero right-side card.

**Seedance / Kling prompt:**
> Lifestyle montage reel, 4:5 vertical, 15–20s loop. Quick-cut B-roll of one young woman across multiple scenes and outfits — all showing her casually, effortlessly using AI on her phone or laptop to handle her life. Warm golden tones throughout, consistent cream and blush palette, slow-motion or 24fps cinematic look.
>
> **Scene 1 — Morning, bedroom.** Oversized white tee, messy bun, still in bed. She's propped on pillows, scrolling her phone with one hand, smirking at something on screen. Coffee mug on nightstand. Soft morning light.
>
> **Scene 2 — Getting ready, bathroom mirror.** Cute matching set, hair half-done. She glances at her phone propped against the mirror, reads something, mouths "oh nice" to herself and keeps getting ready. Warm vanity light.
>
> **Scene 3 — Cafe, midday.** Linen co-ord, small gold hoops. Laptop open, matcha beside her. She types something fast, leans back, looks satisfied — done. Bright natural window light, blurred background.
>
> **Scene 4 — Walking outside.** Casual chic — cream top, wide-leg trousers, sunglasses pushed up. AirPods in, listening to something, smiling to herself. Golden late-afternoon street light. She glances down at her phone and keeps walking.
>
> **Scene 5 — Couch, evening.** Soft matching loungewear, legs tucked under her. Phone in one hand, typing with her thumb, fully relaxed. Warm lamp light, cozy and unhurried.
>
> Consistent color grade: warm amber, cream, blush. Slow zooms or gentle push-ins on each scene. Cuts every 3–4 seconds. The vibe is: she has everything handled and it was barely even effort.

**Format:** MP4, ideally 1080×1350 (4:5), max 10s loop, muted
