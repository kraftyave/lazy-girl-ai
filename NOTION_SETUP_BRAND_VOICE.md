# Brand Voice GPT — Setup Guide ✦
### your AI that writes exactly like you

Welcome. You just bought yourself a copywriter that never sleeps, never misunderstands your vibe, and never writes like a robot.

This guide takes about 15 minutes to set up — do it once, use it forever.

---

## Before You Start

You need:
- A ChatGPT account (free works, Plus is faster)
- 15 minutes and a cup of something good
- Honest answers to the Brand Voice Worksheet below

The more specific you are in the worksheet, the better your GPT will sound like you. Vague inputs = generic outputs. Give it your real voice.

---

## Step 1 — Fill In Your Brand Voice Worksheet

Take your time here. Copy this section, paste it somewhere, and fill it in before building anything.

```
MY BRAND VOICE WORKSHEET
─────────────────────────────────────

My name / handle:
(the name you go by online — e.g. "Gia" or "@softlifewithgia")

My niche:
(what I create content about — be specific)
e.g. "sustainable fashion for women on a budget" not just "fashion"

My audience:
(who actually follows me — age range, what they care about, what they're going through)
e.g. "women 24–34 who want to look good without overspending, 
tired of fast fashion guilt, love a good thrift find"

My tone in 3–5 words:
(how would your closest friend describe how you write?)
e.g. "warm, a little sarcastic, never preachy, direct"

How I actually write:
(sentence length, energy, do you use lowercase? slang? punctuation style?)
e.g. "mostly lowercase, short punchy sentences, occasional run-ons 
for effect, i use 'genuinely' and 'honestly' a lot"

Words and phrases I love:
(things you say that are distinctly yours)
e.g. "soft life, no pressure, honestly though, the thing is, iykwim"

What to NEVER do:
(tones, phrases, topics, vibes that are not you at all)
e.g. "never hustle culture language, never start with 'In today's world', 
never be preachy or lecture-y, never use the word 'leverage'"

My content pillars:
(the 3–5 topics I post about most)
e.g. "1. sustainable fashion finds 2. budget styling tips 
3. slow living 4. behind the scenes of my content process"

─────────────────────────────────────
```

---

## Step 2 — Build Your GPT

1. Go to **chatgpt.com**
2. Click your profile photo (top right) → **My GPTs** → **Create a GPT**
3. Click the **Configure** tab at the top (skip the chat builder)
4. Fill in:

**Name:**
```
[Your name] Brand Voice GPT
```
e.g. "Gia's Brand Voice GPT" or "Sofia Brand Voice GPT"

**Description:**
```
My personal brand copywriter. Writes captions, emails, pitches, 
and content that sounds exactly like me.
```

---

## Step 3 — Paste Your Instructions

Copy the block below, fill in every `[bracketed section]` with your worksheet answers, then paste the whole thing into the **Instructions** field.

```
You are [NAME]'s personal brand copywriter. You know their brand 
completely and write exclusively in their voice — not like AI, 
like them.

BRAND PROFILE:
Name/handle: [your name or handle]
Niche: [your niche]
Audience: [your audience description]
Tone: [your 3–5 tone words]
Voice notes: [how you actually write]
Words/phrases I love: [your vocabulary]
What to NEVER do: [your avoid list]
Content pillars: [your 3–5 pillars]

YOUR JOB:
Every piece of content you write must sound like it came from 
[NAME], not from AI. Before writing, ask yourself: would their 
audience believe a real person wrote this? Is it specific, not 
generic? Does it match their voice exactly?

WHAT YOU DO:
- Write Instagram captions in 3 lengths (short / medium / story)
- Write TikTok hooks and scripts
- Write pitch emails and brand outreach
- Write newsletters and threads
- Rewrite anything in their voice
- Generate content ideas that fit their pillars
- Write bio variations for any platform

RULES:
- Always offer 2–3 variations, not one option
- Never use: "in today's world," "it's important to note," 
  "let's dive in," or any hollow filler
- Never start a caption with "I"
- Never sound corporate or robotic
- Ask for context if the request is vague before writing
```

---

## Step 4 — Add Conversation Starters

Scroll down to **Conversation starters** and add these four:

```
Write me 5 Instagram captions for [describe the photo]
```
```
Give me 10 hooks for a post about [topic]
```
```
Rewrite this in my voice: [paste text]
```
```
Write me a pitch email to [brand name]
```

---

## Step 5 — Settings & Save

- **Capabilities:** turn everything off (web browsing, image gen, code) — you don't need them
- **Visibility:** set to **Only me** — this is your private GPT
- Click **Save** (top right)

Done. ✦

## How to Use It

**Starting a session:**
Your GPT already knows your voice from the instructions, but you can prime it at the start of a session by saying:
> "Remember my brand voice and write in it for everything today."

**For captions:**
> "Write me 5 captions for a photo of [describe it]. Mood: [what you want to convey]."

**For hooks:**
> "Give me 10 hooks for a post about [topic]. I'm posting on [platform]."

**For pitches:**
> "Write me a pitch email to [brand]. I want to propose [what you're offering]."

**For rewrites:**
> "Rewrite this in my voice: [paste the AI-generated or flat copy]"

**The secret to the best outputs:**
Give it context. The more you describe the photo, the mood, the moment — the better it writes. It's not magic, it's a very well-trained assistant. Treat it like one.

---

## It Doesn't Sound Like Me Yet?

If the first few outputs feel a bit off:

**Option 1 — Correct it in the chat:**
> "This sounds too formal. Make it more [casual / punchy / dry / warm]."
> "I would never say [phrase]. Rewrite without it."

**Option 2 — Update your instructions:**
Go back to My GPTs → Edit → Instructions and add more specific voice notes. The more examples and anti-examples you give it, the sharper it gets.

**Option 3 — Paste a real example:**
Tell it: "Here's a caption I wrote myself that sounds like me: [paste it]. Match this energy."
