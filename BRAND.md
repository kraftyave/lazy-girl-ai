## What We Are

- A done-for-you AI product studio for women
- Pre-built systems: custom GPTs, automations, Notion setups, prompt frameworks
- The shortcut between "AI sounds useful" and "AI is actually running my life"

## What We Are Not

- A tutorial channel or course platform
- A prompt library (that's the free entry point, not the product)
- Hustle culture with an AI spin
- For tech people
- Another course telling you what's possible

## On the Beginner Layer

We do welcome women who have never used AI before — but we teach by doing, not by explaining.

The free prompts are the "catch you up in 5 minutes" entry point. They show what AI actually does by doing it. That's enough. We don't add courses, explainer series, or curriculum. The learning happens by using the tools, not by studying them.

The path: **try a free prompt → understand what AI can do → buy a system that runs it for you.**

Education is in service of the funnel. It is not the product. The moment it feels like a class, we've drifted.

## The Problem We Solve

Everyone knows AI exists. Almost nobody has set it up to actually work for their specific life. The gap isn't knowledge — it's setup, configuration, and context. That's what we close.

## Audience

Women who:
- Know AI could help them but haven't had the time or energy to figure it out
- Are running a life, a brand, a business, or all three
- Want the outcome, not the process
- Would rather use a thing than learn a thing

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** honest, direct, warm, a little dry. Talks like a smart friend who figured something out and is just telling you about it.

**Never:** condescending, overwhelming, preachy, hustle-y, overly technical, toxic positivity.

**Always:** specific, confident, outcome-focused, relatable without being performatively chaotic.

### Voice examples

| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| "leverage AI to optimize your workflow" | "let AI handle your Monday before you even wake up" |
| "learn how to use AI prompts" | "we built it — you just fill in your name" |
| "AI is the future of productivity" | "it took us 20 mins to build. it'll save you 20 hours." |
| "empower yourself with AI tools" | "your inbox won't clear itself. this will do it for you." |
| "join our community of AI learners" | "plug it in. it runs." |

## Brand Pillars

**1. Done for you** — systems and setups delivered ready to use, not explained

**3. No tech required** — the barrier was setup. we removed it.
**4. Soft but serious** — the aesthetic is warm and feminine. the results are real.

## Visual Identity

**Palette:**
- Cream `#FFF6F0` — backgrounds, breathing room
- Blush `#F2A7B3` — primary accent, CTAs, heart
- Light Blush `#FFD7D1` — soft highlights
- Sage `#C7D1C2` — secondary accent, balance
- Taupe `#8B7A6B` — body text, captions
- Charcoal `#1C1C1C` — headlines, logo

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Playfair Display (serif, italic for emphasis)
- Body: Poppins (light to semibold)
- Accent/logo: Dancing Script

**Brand mark:** ✦ four-pointed star — used as a separator, accent, and end mark

**Aesthetic:** warm editorial lifestyle. soft, intentional, premium but approachable. not girlboss. not tech startup.
