## the positioning

**you are not an AI account. you are a lifestyle account where AI is the mechanism.**

the niche isn't "AI tips." it's the shortcut to the life lazy girls actually want —  
and AI is how they get there.

> **the one-line positioning:**
> "the girl who has it together — and AI is why"

the content shows the OUTCOME (organised life, clear inbox, content done in 20 min)  
not the tool. the tool is the backstory.

## the dual brand model

### track 1 — faceless brand
runs 24/7 with minimal effort. AI-powered. no camera required.

- Twitter/X threads
- Instagram carousels
- Pinterest pins
- YouTube Shorts (screen recordings, voiceover)
- newsletter
- blog posts

**the content machine (set and forget):**
1. write one newsletter issue/week in your voice
2. run it through the content repurpose workflow (the product you sell)
3. outputs: 5 tweets · 1 carousel · 1 YT Short script · 3 pins · 1 LinkedIn post
4. schedule. done. you're posting 10+ pieces of content from one session.

the fact that you use your own product publicly IS the marketing.

---

### track 2 — face-led brand (TikTok + Reels)
you, talking. casual. relatable. the lazy girl who figured it out.

**on not being telegenic:**  
you don't need to be. the lazy girl brand specifically benefits from NOT being over-produced.  
authenticity beats polish on TikTok every single time — the algorithm rewards watch time and  
relatability, not beauty. the creators who look "too perfect" actually underperform now.

what you need instead:
- good lighting (a $20 ring light or a window)
- decent audio (AirPods mic is fine)
- a hook in the first 2 seconds
- captions (CapCut auto-generates them)
- your personality — which is already the brand voice

**the format that works without being telegenic:**
- talk to camera like you're telling your best friend something useful
- POV-style: camera at eye level, casual background, no set dressing required
- the "lazy girl explaining AI" energy is EXACTLY the brand — you're not performing expertise,  
  you're sharing what's actually working for you
- one idea per video. short. done.

---

### 02 — the lazy girl tutorial
short, specific, zero jargon. you're the translator between AI and normal women.

**formats:** TikTok, Reels, YouTube Short, carousel, newsletter
**examples:**
- "how to use ChatGPT as a personal assistant (actually useful)"
- "the exact prompt I use to write a week of captions in 10 min"
- "how I automated my email so I never have to sort it again"
- "3 AI tools that actually changed my daily life (not ChatGPT)"

**the lazy girl tutorial formula:**
1. hook: the outcome (not the tool)
2. quick context: why it mattered to me
3. the actual how-to: 3 steps max
4. result: what my life looks like now
5. CTA: link in bio / newsletter

---

### 03 — hot takes / lazy girl verdict
react to AI news in brand voice. this is the content machine pillar.

**formats:** Twitter/X (primary), TikTok reaction, newsletter section
**examples:**
- "everyone's talking about [new AI thing] — here's if it actually matters for your life"
- "lazy girl verdict: is [new AI tool] worth it? I tested it so you don't have to"
- "overrated AI things you can stop using in 2026"
- "the AI update this week that actually changes something for normal people"

**the machine setup:**
- scrape AI news daily (use a tool like Feedly or a Claude prompt)
- feed headlines to Claude with your brand voice prompt
- get 5 Twitter takes + 1 newsletter paragraph per session
- review, edit, post

## the voice

**brand marks:** ✦ and ♡ — use them. they're recognisable.

**tone:** warm, self-aware, a little dry. not hustle culture. not tech bro.  
the girl who figured it out and is happy to share — not selling you a course.

**do say:**
- "so you don't have to"
- "here's what actually works"
- "I tested it / I use it daily"
- "lazy girl verdict"
- "you're welcome ♡"

**don't say:**
- "grind" / "hustle" / "crush it"
- "10X your productivity"
- "this will change your life" (let the outcome speak)
- overly technical anything

## the AI content machine — setup guide

**what you need:**
- Claude (for writing in your voice)
- CapCut (for captions + editing)
- Later or Buffer (for scheduling)
- Feedly or similar (for AI news aggregation)

**the weekly workflow:**
1. **monday morning (30 min):** write the newsletter issue draft
2. **monday afternoon (10 min):** run it through the content repurpose prompt → get 5 tweets, 1 carousel outline, 1 short script
3. **record / create (1-2 hours):** batch 3-5 videos + design 2 carousels
4. **schedule everything (20 min):** queue the week
5. **done.** the machine runs.

**the brand voice prompt for Claude:**
```
you are writing content for lazy girl ai — a lifestyle brand that helps women use AI 
to run their lives without the technical overwhelm. 

voice: warm, self-aware, a little dry. not hustle culture. 
uses ✦ and ♡ as brand marks. short sentences. no em dashes.
speaks to: women 20-35 who want their life to run smoothly and look good doing it.
never sounds: technical, bro-ish, salesy, or like a productivity guru.

[paste your newsletter / idea here]

write: [5 tweets / 1 carousel / 1 TikTok script / etc.]
```

## on showing up on camera

you don't need to be telegenic. you need to be REAL.

the lazy girl brand is specifically built for someone who is:
- smart but not performative about it
- figured it out and is sharing casually
- not trying to be an influencer — just being honest

**practical setup:**
- film on your iPhone, vertical
- sit near a window (free lighting)
- AirPods for audio
- wear whatever you'd wear anyway — the lazy girl aesthetic IS the effortless look
- talk like you're explaining something to a friend, not presenting to a classroom

**the hook formula (first 2 seconds decide everything):**
- "I stopped doing [thing] manually and here's what happened"
- "nobody told me you could use AI for [this]"
- "the lazy girl way to [desired outcome]"
- "POV: AI just did [thing] for you"

**the formula for a video that performs without being polished:**
1. hook (2 sec) — outcome-first, not "hi guys"
2. context (5 sec) — why you cared
3. the thing (20-40 sec) — the actual useful bit, fast
4. result (5 sec) — what changed
5. CTA (3 sec) — "link in bio" / "newsletter below" / "follow for more"

total: 35-55 seconds. no fluff. no intro. no outro music that makes people scroll.
