## DAY 8 — "why your AI answers are bad (and how to fix it)"

**COVER (01/07)**
Headline: why your AI answers are bad
Subtext: and exactly how to fix it ✦

---

**SLIDE 01 — too vague**
Headline: you're being too vague
Subtext: "help me" is not a prompt
Body: "write me something about my job" tells AI nothing. it doesn't know your role, your goal, your tone, or what done looks like. vague in = vague out. always.
Fix: tell it what you need as if you're briefing a smart assistant who just started today.

---

**SLIDE 02 — no context**
Headline: you're not giving it context
Subtext: AI knows nothing about you unless you tell it
Body: it doesn't know your industry, your situation, your relationship, or your history. every time you open a new chat, you're starting fresh with someone who knows nothing.
Fix: start with "I am [role/situation]. I'm trying to [goal]. Here's the relevant background: [2-3 sentences]."

---

**SLIDE 03 — not specifying format**
Headline: you're not telling it how to respond
Subtext: format matters more than you think
Body: do you want a bullet list? a script? a table? a short paragraph? a full email draft? if you don't say, AI picks something. it's often not what you wanted.
Fix: end every prompt with "Give me [format]." example: "Give me a 5-bullet list." or "Write this as a short email, under 150 words."

---

**SLIDE 04 — giving up**
Headline: you're giving up after the first answer
Subtext: the first answer is the starting point
Body: AI gives you a draft. you respond. you edit together. "make this more direct" · "remove the formal tone" · "shorten this" · "actually, I changed my situation, add this." that's the real workflow.
Fix: treat it like a collaboration. the second and third answers are almost always better.

---

**SLIDE 05 — the fix**
Headline: the lazy girl fix for all of this
Subtext: one formula
Body: "I am [who you are]. I need [task]. Context: [2-3 sentences]. Give me [format]."
Example: "I am a 25yo product designer. I need to write an email asking for a raise. Context: I've been here 2 years, took on 3 new responsibilities. Give me a script that's confident but not aggressive, under 5 sentences."
